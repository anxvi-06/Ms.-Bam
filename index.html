A Letter Just for You 💌

Some things are better written than said.

Tap the envelope to open ♡

My dearest Ms. Bam,

I don't think you realise just how much you're missed. It's funny how someone can become such a special part of your days without even knowing it.

You were never just another teacher to me. There was something about your presence, your smile, and the way you made ordinary days feel a little brighter.

I know people move on to new chapters, and I'm genuinely happy that you're beginning yours. But a tiny selfish part of me still wishes I could have just one more class with you.

I hope you're smiling a lot, laughing at the smallest things, and being surrounded by all the kindness you deserve.

And please don't forget that somewhere, there's a student who still thinks you're the cutest, sweetest teacher ever.

Thank you for being you, Ms. Bam. I miss you more than these little words could ever explain.

With love, Anavia ♡

WORDS FROM THE HEART

A Little Poem for You 🌷

Some people come and quietly stay,
Even when life takes them away.
Some leave a room, yet leave behind,
A thousand little joys to find.
The days moved on, the seasons flew,
But something stayed that felt like you.
A gentle laugh, a familiar smile,
That made the hardest days worthwhile.
And though the halls may feel the same,
There's something missing, just your name.
For some are remembered for what they do,
But I'll remember how it felt with you.
So here's a thought, both small and true,
Some days still feel a little less without you.
♡

— Anavia Parveen
